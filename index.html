Gurutva Patle

I am a PhD researcher at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) Bangalore, working under the supervision of Prof. Rajiv Soundararajan.

My research focuses on computer vision, neural rendering, and 3D graphics, with a particular emphasis on sparse-input novel view synthesis using 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) and neural field representations.

I work on problems involving 3D understanding, neural reconstruction, and realistic rendering. My projects aim to improve the efficiency, sparse-input performance, and real-world deployment of neural radiance fields and 3D Gaussian Splatting for multi-camera capture systems.

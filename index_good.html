My broader research aims to build systems that can accurately reconstruct and render the 3D world from limited or imperfect visual information. I am particularly interested in efficient 3D representations, radiance field acceleration, and view-consistent scene generation.

AD-GS: Alternating Densification for Sparse-Input 3D Gaussian Splatting

Authors:
Gurutva Patle | Nilay Girgaonkar | Nagabhushan Somraj | Rajiv Soundararajan
🐙 Project Page 📄 Paper 💻 Code

Abstract: 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has shown impressive results in real-time novel view synthesis but often struggles under sparse-view settings, producing floaters, inaccurate geometry, and overfitting. A key cause is uncontrolled densification, where Gaussians are rapidly added without guidance, harming geometry.

We propose AD-GS, a framework alternating between high and low densification phases. High densification focuses on aggressive expansion to capture fine details, while low densification prunes Gaussians and regularizes geometry using pseudo-view consistency and edge-aware depth smoothness.

AD-GS improves rendering quality and geometric consistency across challenging datasets.
